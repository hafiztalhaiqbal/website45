Can Fluvo support an existing facility?+

Yes. Our service scope includes system upgrades, retrofits, troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, and energy performance assessments.

Can you support both design and installation?+

Fluvo provides support from engineering design and equipment selection through installation, testing, commissioning, and handover. The agreed scope depends on your project requirements.

What should I share for a project enquiry?+

Please share your company name, project location, facility type, required services, and intended timeline. Available drawings or a scope of work help us understand the requirement.

Do you assess energy efficiency?+

Yes. Our services include energy audits, utility consumption analysis, HVAC and chiller assessments, compressed-air optimization, and technical feasibility analysis.
