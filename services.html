CAPABILITY 01

HVAC Systems & Ventilation

We design, install, and upgrade heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems to provide comfortable, safe, and energy-efficient indoor environments.

  • HVAC system design and load calculations
  • Chiller plants, AHUs, FCUs, and VRF/VRV systems
  • Industrial ventilation and exhaust systems
  • Fresh-air systems and evaporative cooling
  • HVAC ducting, piping, and insulation
  • Testing, commissioning, and preventive maintenance

Mechanical Utilities & Industrial Piping

We provide mechanical infrastructure solutions that support reliable industrial operations, efficient utility distribution, and continuous production.

  • Compressed-air systems and distribution networks
  • Steam generation and distribution systems
  • Natural gas and SNG distribution networks
  • Chilled-water and cooling-water systems
  • Thermal oil and hot-water systems
  • Pumps, piping, valves, and equipment installation
  • Mechanical equipment erection and commissioning

CAPABILITY 03

Plumbing & Water Management

We develop dependable plumbing and water distribution systems designed to meet facility requirements while supporting efficient water use and reliable operation.

  • Domestic and industrial plumbing
  • Raw-water, drinking-water, and RO-water networks
  • Water storage tanks and pumping systems
  • Drainage and wastewater piping
  • Hot- and cold-water distribution
  • Pump selection, installation, and commissioning

  • Fire hydrant and hose-reel networks
  • Firewater piping and pumping systems

  • Fire protection system installation and testing
  • Inspection, maintenance, and system upgrades
  • Coordination with fire alarm and detection specialists

  • Testing, commissioning, and troubleshooting
